# Art direction

## Visual goal

Fresh, bright, soft, premium, and restrained. The image should feel inviting without becoming toy-like or oversaturated. Large forms carry the composition; small detail is reserved for focal areas. Materials are clean with moderate roughness, broad highlights, and limited metallic accents.

## Palette

- Sky: pale blue with warm horizon haze.
- Ground: sage and mint families rather than neon green.
- Road: blue-gray charcoal, never near-black.
- Architecture: warm cream and light stone.
- Accents: muted coral, cyan, lavender, sand, rose, and soft yellow.
- Emission: used only for pickups, boosts, and state feedback.

Global saturation is slightly reduced. Contrast comes from value separation, shape, lighting, and selective emission rather than extreme chroma.

## Shape language

- Karts: rounded wedge forms, low center of gravity, compact cabin, large readable wheels.
- Track: generous radii, clear edge bands, smooth vertical transitions, broad sightlines.
- Props: simplified trees and clouds built from large soft masses.
- UI: calm translucent panels, large numerals, minimal borders, generous spacing.

## Asset rule

The procedural prototype is a blockout, not permission to copy another game's forms. Future production assets must be original or properly licensed. Free assets may be used only when their license permits commercial redistribution in a game and the notice file is updated.
